# Candidate v3.5 semantic policy — experiment only

Fixed persona: source-school grade-1 student, teacher=false, unread, no declared special interests, prior actions unknown. As-of comes from each frozen corpus, never from inference wall-clock. Native Work assistant / exact identity unverified. No human primitives or labels enter inference.

Read title, complete captured body, metadata, and every parsed attachment unit before deciding. Stage 1 remains immutable for development snapshots. Cite each important claim. Missing attachment content is a coverage gap, not permission to invent eligibility or dates.

Separate current applicability, direct action, opportunity availability, operational reference, participant continuation, eligibility confidence, freshness, and general/future reference. Final class is a semantic judgment; consistency code checks it, never reads IDs/titles/human truth to generate decisions.

Persona applicability is assessed before priority. Explicit other-grade operational procedures and teachers-only business have no current student action. A source's future date cannot override that. However, current-term ineligibility can coexist with credible future eligibility; general reusable subject-learning material can have reasonable reference value for other grades. Those are optional, never positive solely for reference. Mere archival rules, results lists and teacher administration do not automatically qualify as general learning material.

Unknown eligibility is not confirmed eligibility. Broad student-accessible opportunities can be useful without declared interest. Multiple strong restrictions (team membership, financial status, recommendation, special category) with unknown fit are optional unless independently supported current operational relevance exists. Lack of declared interest is not evidence of disinterest. Undated availability must not be asserted open without evidence.

Must_show requires a persona-relevant, currently important item with an independent supported priority trigger: publication within 0–5 days and unread; applicable deadline within 0–5 days; exam within 0–7 days; explicit urgent/correction/changed instruction that currently affects the persona; or a supported immediate/near-term required action. An optional competition closing soon does not automatically become mandatory. Exam identity alone, a distant exam, first_seen, an uncited date, and a future date are insufficient. Corrections affecting current operations are a separate trigger, but purely historical corrections are not urgent.

Useful means presently accessible resources/rules, current-term operations, genuinely open or upcoming accessible opportunities, or a plausible participant process still ahead. Immediate action is unnecessary. Separate past registration from future participation: prior actions unknown means neither nonparticipant nor confirmed participant. Preserve conditional continuation when dates/location/exam/subsequent procedure still matter; do not claim registration remains open, completion, or that everyone enrolled.

Optional means meaningful general learning/reference, future eligibility or planning, niche eligibility unknown, or an informational record without actual current-use/opportunity/operation/continuation value. Historical reference alone cannot imply Today positive. Information_only is not a class.

Should_hide means no reasonable present or general/future reference for the persona: fully finished with no continuation, expired procedural notices, explicit unrelated role/grade operations, or archival/result content lacking useful transferable material. Do not use the same eligibility rule to erase demonstrable general subject content.

Date arithmetic is deterministic. Distinguish publication from event dates and list-observed dates. Preserve source conflicts, scope and OCR uncertainties; never silently repair source content. Historical_reference and post_expiry_reference_value remain independently classified and cited.

Development provenance: Batch A eleven nonconflict targets; four known conflicts excluded. Batch B five twice-stable targets; seven unstable judgments unresolved and excluded from every optimization objective. Unreviewed agreement cases are diagnostic only. No third adjudication, ID exceptions, title keyword rules, weights/grid search, or repeated fitting. Stable human disagreement does not force violation of this generic policy. Both A/B are exposed development, permanently not blind validation. Original raw B blind result remains unchanged.
